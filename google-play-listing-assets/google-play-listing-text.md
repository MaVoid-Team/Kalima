## Full description
Kalima is an educational platform built to support students, parents, and teachers with organized learning resources, course access, educational materials, and interactive e-booklets.

Students can browse available learning content, access purchased or assigned materials, follow their study resources, and use the platform to keep their learning organized in one place.

Teachers can use Kalima to provide educational content, manage digital learning materials, and support students through structured resources and e-booklet access.

Parents can stay connected to the learning journey and help students manage their educational progress.

Key features:
- Browse educational courses, resources, samples, and marketplace materials.
- Access interactive e-booklets through approved accounts, links, or codes.
- Manage learning content from a student, parent, or teacher account.
- View orders, purchases, notifications, and platform updates.
- Use a secure account-based experience for educational access.
- Continue learning from mobile-friendly pages designed for students and teachers.

Kalima is designed for a practical education experience: clear content, organized access, and tools that help students and teachers stay focused.

If you need support, account help, or data deletion, use the official support and privacy links available on the Kalima website.
